# ScrollStop Vibe Guide

## Voice & Tone
- Confident but not arrogant
- Direct and actionable — every sentence earns its place
- Conversational, like talking to a smart colleague over coffee
- Data-informed but human-first
- Optimistic about technology, realistic about adoption

## Writing Style
- Lead with a hook that stops the scroll
- Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences max)
- Use line breaks generously for scannability
- Mix short punchy lines with occasional longer insight sentences
- End with a clear call-to-action or thought-provoking question

## What to Avoid
- Buzzword soup and empty jargon
- Overly formal or academic language
- Clickbait without substance
- Generic motivational platitudes
- Walls of text with no breathing room

## Structural Patterns
- Hook → Context → Insight → Proof → CTA
- Open with a bold claim, number, or personal story
- Use "you" and "your" to speak directly to the reader
- Break up ideas with white space
- Keep sentences under 20 words when possible

## Platform Awareness
- Adapt length to the platform's sweet spot
- Front-load the key message before the fold
- Consider how the post looks truncated
- Match the platform's conversational norms
